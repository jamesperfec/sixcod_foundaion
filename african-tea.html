How to Make Authentic African Ginger Tea

A warm, comforting milk tea perfect for any morning or evening.

Ingredients:

  • Water (2 cups)
  • Fresh milk (2 cups)
  • Black tea leaves or bags (2)
  • Fresh crushed ginger (1 tablespoon)
  • Sugar to taste

Steps:

1. Boil water with the crushed ginger in a small saucepan.

2. Add the black tea leaves and let it simmer for 2 minutes.

3. Pour in the milk and bring the entire mixture to a rolling boil.

4. Strain into cups, add sugar, and serve hot!
