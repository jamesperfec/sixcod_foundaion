Spicy Rwandan Akabanga Potatoes

A fiery, flavorful local favorite using Rwanda's legendary Akabanga chili oil.

Ingredients:

  • Irish potatoes (4 large, cubed)
  • Onion (1 medium, chopped)
  • Garlic (2 cloves, minced)
  • Tomato paste (1 tablespoon)
  • Akabanga Chili Oil (2 to 4 drops)
  • Salt and oil for frying

Steps:

1. Peel and cube the potatoes, then par-boil them in salted water for 5 minutes until slightly soft.

2. Heat oil in a pan and fry the potatoes until they get a beautiful golden, crispy outer skin. Remove and set aside.

3. In the same pan, saute your chopped onions and minced garlic until fragrant.

4. Stir in the tomato paste, toss your crispy potatoes back into the pan, and coat them thoroughly.

5. Remove from heat, carefully add your drops of **Akabanga chili oil**, mix well, and serve steaming hot!
